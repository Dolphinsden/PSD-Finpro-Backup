Place your downloaded shareware DOOM1.WAD (~4,196,020 bytes) in this folder
named exactly:

    DOOM1.WAD

You can download the official freeware/shareware DOOM1.WAD (v1.9) from:
  - https://distro.ibiblio.org/slitaz/sources/packages/d/doom1.wad
  - https://archive.org/details/DoomsharewareEpisode

Once DOOM1.WAD is here, run:
    python scripts/setup_doom_sources.py
    vitis -s scripts/build_vitis.py
